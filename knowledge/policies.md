# Bookly Customer Policies

Bookly is an online bookstore based in Stockholm, Sweden, selling printed books and e-books.

## Shipping

### Where we ship
- **Sweden, Denmark and Finland:** standard and express shipping.
- **Norway:** standard shipping only. Books are VAT-free in Norway, so no VAT is added to your order. Because Norway is outside the EU, packages go through customs, which can occasionally add a day or two to delivery.
- **Rest of the EU:** standard shipping only.
- We do not currently ship outside the EU and Norway.

### Delivery times and costs

| Destination | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| Sweden | 2–4 business days, 49 SEK | 1–2 business days, 99 SEK |
| Denmark, Finland | 3–5 business days, 79 SEK | 2–3 business days, 149 SEK |
| Norway | 4–7 business days, 99 SEK | Not available |
| Rest of the EU | 5–10 business days, 129 SEK | Not available |

- Free standard shipping within Sweden on orders over 399 SEK.
- Orders placed before 14:00 on a business day are shipped the same day.
- Delivery times are estimates. Delays caused by the carrier are outside Bookly's control, but we will help track the package.

### Tracking
- A tracking number is emailed when the order ships.
- If a package has not arrived 10 business days after shipping, contact support and we will investigate with the carrier.

## Orders

### Changing or cancelling an order
- An order can be changed or cancelled free of charge until it has shipped.
- Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, but it can be returned under the return policy below.

## Returns and refunds

### Return window
- Printed books can be returned within **30 days of delivery**.
- Books must be unused and in the condition they were received.
- **E-books cannot be returned** once downloaded, except if the file is faulty.

### Damaged, faulty or wrong items
- If a book arrives damaged or Bookly sent the wrong item, contact support within 30 days of delivery.
- Bookly covers return shipping in these cases and sends a replacement or a full refund, at the customer's choice.

### How returns work
- Returns are started through customer support. The customer receives a return number and a prepaid return label by email.
- For returns where the customer has changed their mind, a return shipping fee of 49 SEK is deducted from the refund.

### Refunds
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of Bookly receiving the returned item.
- Original shipping costs are refunded only if the item was damaged, faulty or wrong.

## Accounts

### Password reset
- Customers can reset their password through the "Forgot password" link on the login page.
- Support can also send a password reset link to the email address registered on the account.
- For security reasons, support staff never see, ask for or set customers' passwords, and the reset link is only ever sent to the registered email address.

### Changing account details
- Name, address and email can be changed in the account settings after logging in.

## Contacting a human

- Customer support specialists are available Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 (Swedish time).
- Requests outside these hours are answered the next business day.
